# What Changed

The confirmed earlier thesis remains unchanged. The revised draft keeps its exact title and focuses its research argument on evidence review with the capstone agent.

## Chapter reduction map

| Earlier material | Treatment in the revised draft | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chapters 2 through 5: problem, MOSA principles, policy, and evidence | Retain the strongest material in the problem and foundation chapters | Establish the need for review and the relevant rules |
| Chapters 6 and 7: original implementation and results | Summarize the original study, then develop the capstone comparison | Keep the demonstrated foundation while narrowing the new question |
| Chapters 8 through 15: architecture, operations, security, testing, and metrics | Combine evidence tracing, validation, guards, and human review | Explain the implemented review process without a broad enterprise manual |
| Enterprise implementation, governance, acquisition, workforce, and the 36 month roadmap | Remove as standalone chapters | These topics do not answer the new evaluation question |
| Chapters 19 through 24: domain profiles, legal issues, financial models, organizational patterns, mission scenarios, and MBSE | Keep only context needed to understand prototype limits | Avoid conclusions unsupported by the new study |
| Extensive scoring equations, catalogs, and technical appendices | Keep measurements used in the comparison; move technical detail to the companion | Preserve inspectability while reducing document length |
| Chapters 25 and 26 | Rewrite conclusions and future work around recorded findings | Let the evidence determine the result |

MOSA means Modular Open Systems Approach. MBSE means Model Based Systems Engineering, which uses structured models to describe a system. These wider subjects can support future work, but the revised research question concerns review behavior and evidence.
