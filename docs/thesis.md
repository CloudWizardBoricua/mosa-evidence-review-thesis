## The question and answer to look for

**When does an agent help a person review MOSA evidence, and what checks are needed to keep its recommendations reliable?** MOSA means Modular Open Systems Approach.

The thesis separates three claims: a fixed harness can detect known defects; an agent can choose checks and explain findings; and fixed rules plus human review are still necessary to control recommendations. The conclusion should follow the recorded comparison, even if explicit rules offer the same benefit at lower cost.

!!! question "Amber: what does readiness mean here?"
    Readiness means that the supplied evidence passed the required checks in this research profile. A profile is the particular set of rules used for a review. Readiness does not mean that a complete defense system is certified or approved for deployment.
